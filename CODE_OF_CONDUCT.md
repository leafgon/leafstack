# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project. Participants
are expected to communicate respectfully, give actionable technical feedback,
assume good faith while still protecting users, and respect privacy and
confidentiality.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sharing
credentials and private graph data without authorization.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or
its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Leafgon organization maintainers
through GitHub. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive
details. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical, with
conflicts of interest avoided where possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
